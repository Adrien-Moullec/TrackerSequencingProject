Arachno SoundFont: Often cited as a superior, high-quality replacement for default soundfonts, offering rich sounds, particularly for rock and orchestral, according to users.
GeneralUser GS: A "Genuinely Usable" soundfont, great for general MIDI playback and producing.
FluidR3_GM: A widely used, comprehensive, and well-balanced General MIDI soundfont.
8MBGMSFX & Airfont: Known as balanced, high-quality, smaller-sized soundfonts, perfect for when memory is limited.
Timbres of Heaven: A comprehensive set designed to be Roland GS compatible. 

Where to Find and Download SF2 Files
Soundfonts4U: Highly recommended for, especially acoustic guitars.
Musical Artifacts: A large, community-driven database for finding specific, user-recommended soundfonts.
GitHub "Awesome Soundfonts": An organized list of reputable free soundfonts.
SynthFont: A classic repository of links to various GM and specialized SF2 libraries. 

Specialized Recommendations
Piano: The Maestro Concert Grand is frequently recommended for high-quality piano sound.
Orchestral: Many users turn to specialized collections on sites like Musical Artifacts or KVR Audio.
Retro/Gaming: Look for NES or SNES specific soundfonts on Musical Artifacts for retro game sounds.
